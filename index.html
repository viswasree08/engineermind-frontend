Stage 3: Solver Configuration & Numerical Schemes

Prescribe governing equations and schemes based on flow regime and Reynolds number.

Recommended Turbulence Model:
k-omega SST (Menter)

External aerodynamics involves adverse pressure gradients. Standard k-epsilon overpredicts turbulent viscosity and delays separation. SST k-omega resolves near-wall shear stresses accurately.

Spatial Discretization Scheme:
Second-Order Upwind (Momentum & Turbulence)

Warning: Never use First-Order Upwind for final aerodynamic drag reports; artificial numerical diffusion introduces false drag.
