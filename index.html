Senior Engineer Rule: Blunt Trailing Edge Defeaturing

Recommended Turbulence Model: k-omega SST (Menter)

External aerodynamics involves boundary layers subjected to adverse pressure gradients. Standard k-epsilon overpredicts turbulent viscosity, delaying separation. SST k-omega resolves near-wall shear stresses accurately.

Spatial Discretization Scheme: Second-Order Upwind (Momentum & Turbulence)

Warning: Never use First-Order Upwind for final aerodynamic drag reports; artificial numerical diffusion introduces false drag.
